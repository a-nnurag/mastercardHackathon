TITLE: Visa Threats Report: As Network Security Strengthens, Criminals Accelerate Shift to AI-Enabled Social Engineering (Spring 2026 Biannual Threats Report)

Criminals are leveraging AI to scale sophisticated scams that manipulate victims into authorizing payments themselves: "Criminals are increasingly targeting people rather than technology, using deception, urgency and AI-enabled tools to exploit trust." AI has democratized fraud capabilities: "What once required deep technical skill can now be executed with a prompt."

Scam statistics: from July to December 2025, Visa detected approximately $1 billion in scam-related activity, making scams the largest category of consumer payment fraud. Attacks typically involve impersonation of trusted brands, manufactured urgency, and deception tactics rather than technical breaches.

Broader trends: traditional fraud declining (device token fraud down 9.6% YoY, network-level security improving); scams accelerating as the dominant consumer threat, replacing direct system attacks; AI weaponization on both attacker and defender sides throughout the transaction lifecycle; ransomware incidents up 26% but only 23% of victims paid (record low), reflecting improved recovery capabilities.
